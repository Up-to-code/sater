## Sater Visual Style

Before creating, generating, editing, or approving any Sater visual asset, read and follow [Sater Visual Style](.github/instructions/sater-visual-style.instructions.md).

Reject an asset when it contains multiple competing subjects, perspective, 3D depth, a scene, ornamental filler, glow-heavy effects, or unnecessary detail. Sater assets are flat 2D graphics with one clear subject.
